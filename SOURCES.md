# Source inventory and use

This inventory records the seven references already in the manuscript and their
roles. It adds no new source-verification claim. See CITATION_AUDIT.md and
NOVELTY_SCOPE.md for access and historical-priority limitations.

| Source | Identifier | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Zachary (2007), *A note on insensitivity in stochastic networks* | DOI 10.1239/jap/1175267175; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611526 | Load-bearing classical stationary theorem. Section 3.1 explicitly supplies the arrival/service-function substitution and balance equations. |
| Bonald and Proutière (2002), *Insensitivity in processor-sharing networks* | DOI 10.1016/S0166-5316(02)00110-4 | Background on network insensitivity, not a proof of freshness insensitivity. |
| Gandarias, Doncel and Assaad (2023), *On the age of information of processor sharing systems* | DOI 10.23919/JCN.2023.000042; inspected manuscript https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02083 | Originating conjecture and model comparison. Attribution refers specifically to Conjecture 1, equations (19)–(20), and Appendix A Table I of the inspected version. Direct journal-DOI opening failed during the audit. |
| Chiariotti (2024), *Age of information analysis for a shared edge computing server* | DOI 10.1109/TCOMM.2024.3412761 | Adjacent shared-server age model and scope comparison. |
| Inoue and Mandjes (2025), *Characterizing the age of information with multiple coexisting data streams* | DOI 10.1109/TIT.2025.3544266 | Adjacent multiple-stream age literature. |
| Liu, Huang, Li and Ji (2021), *Anti-aging scheduling in single-server queues: A systematic and comparative study* | DOI 10.23919/JCN.2021.000005; inspected manuscript https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.04271 | Scheduling and age-model comparison. |
| Redder (2026), *Age-of-information in distributed systems caused by asynchronous computing modeled as parallel renewal processes* | DOI 10.1007/s11222-026-10885-x | Adjacent asynchronous/renewal age setting, not the same processor-sharing queue. |

The supplied 19 October source audit and the bounded 20 October update underpin
the source discussion. Bibliographic existence alone is not evidence that a
theorem applies: the manuscript supplies the load-bearing model comparison.
Third-party papers are cited rather than redistributed. The complete new proofs
and all release-owned finite checks are included in this repository; private
review reports are not necessary to follow them.
