Bounded computational replay environment
Python: 3.14.7
NumPy: 2.4.2
Dependency pin: requirements.txt

Normal replay: python3 -B code/replay.py
Optimized replay: python3 -B -O code/replay.py
Semantic negative control: python3 -B code/check_negative.py

The negative-control wrapper launches optimized Python and requires rejection of
an intentionally incorrect finite-chain expectation. No assertion statement is
needed to enforce the new replay checks.

PDF construction uses Pandoc and Tectonic via code/build_paper.py; these are not
dependencies of the mathematical checks. See TYPESETTING.md.

The environment of the original historical simulation receipts was not fully
recorded. Their preservation does not establish bitwise regeneration.
