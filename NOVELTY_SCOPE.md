The primary originating manuscript remains
https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02083 and the author-hosted copy
https://josudoncel.github.io/publicat/AoI_PS.pdf . It concerns the correct
lossless processor-sharing model. The primary institutional manuscript for
Chiariotti's adjacent shared-edge-server model is
https://www.research.unipd.it/retrieve/d047ec33-9881-4202-96c6-07a854fc8483/Age_of_Information_Analysis_for_a_Shared_Edge_Computing_Server.pdf .
Its periodic request model differs from the fixed-law Poisson queue here.

No equivalent lower-end regularly varying stationary-age theorem was identified
in this bounded update. Search results concerning ordinary sojourn times or
foreground-background sharing do not establish equivalence to freshness under
egalitarian sharing. No claim of exhaustive coverage or absolute priority follows.

Direct opening of the originating journal DOI through the browsing service failed
in this update. The manuscript therefore retains attribution to the inspected
arXiv version and does not claim to have checked the definitive journal text or
all corrections. The queueing theorem is independent of the textual discrepancy.

Classical processor-sharing insensitivity and the product-exponential law are
acknowledged prior ingredients. The regular-variation extension is proved
self-contained in Section 3.6; standard regular-variation terminology is not
claimed as a new method.
