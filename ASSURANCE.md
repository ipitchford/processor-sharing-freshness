# Assurance boundaries

Theorem 1 and Theorem 1a are prose proofs. The classical stationary queue input is
Zachary's cited theorem. The remainder of the age-limit argument is supplied in
full, including all-moment uniform integrability. Computational checks do not
prove those universal statements.

Fresh checks reproduce three finite rational means, pure-power scale algebra,
one logarithmic diagnostic and a 2,000-sample stationary simulation. Both ordinary
and optimized Python runs are used. A corrupted exact mean must be rejected.
The full historical simulations were not rerun for publication.

The strongest exclusions are non-Poisson arrivals, cancellation, positive minimum
service, zero-index lower-end regular variation, nonstationary or varying service
laws, finite buffers and finite-load approximation guarantees. The global
stochastic comparison is restricted to exponential service.

AI-assisted adversarial checking is not independent human peer review.
No formal verification or exhaustive novelty certification is asserted.
